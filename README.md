## 🖌️ Here, strokes don't just sit still

You can choose from all sorts of brushes and styles, from pencils and markers to ink, neon, chalk, stars, dots, and pixel effects. But that's only half the fun. There are also plenty of ways to bring your drawings to life, with wavy, fluid, jittery, stretchy, chaotic, and even glitchy effects. Basically, you can draw a perfectly ordinary line... or make it look like it's having a really rough day.

You can tweak your brush size and color, erase, fill areas, pick colors straight from your drawing, and mix and match tools until you find your own style. This is a place for experimenting. If you end up with a masterpiece, fantastic. If you end up with the weirdest doodle imaginable, that counts too. 😎

## 🎨 Your canvas, your rules

Pick your colors, change the background, and adjust the canvas to suit your vision. Zoom in to work on the tiny details, zoom out to see the whole picture, and pan around so you don't get lost along the way. The idea is to give you plenty of room to experiment without having to wrestle with the tools.

## 🧩 Made a mess? No big deal

You can use layers to keep your artwork organized, control what's visible, and adjust opacity. And, of course, there are undo and redo options, because we all know that sometimes a stroke that seemed like a brilliant idea two seconds ago suddenly doesn't look quite so brilliant anymore.

## 💾 Let's make sure your masterpieces don't disappear

The app saves your work in your browser and lets you manage your projects. You can also export and import projects, so you can open them again and pick up where you left off later. Just keep in mind that local saving depends on your browser and device, so I'd recommend keeping a backup of any important projects. Better safe than sorry, especially when it comes to recreating that one perfect line you accidentally stumbled upon.

## 🎞️ A drawing is cool, but why not make it move?

You can save your creations as PNG images, including ones with transparent backgrounds, or turn them into animated GIFs. Video export is also available, with options to adjust the duration, frame rate, quality, and resolution. You can even use solid-color backgrounds designed for chroma keying, in case you want to use your creations in other projects.

More demanding exports depend on your device's processing power and browser, so if you try to export a massive video and your computer starts questioning all its life choices, try lowering the resolution. 😂

## 🤝 What if we drew together?

There's also an online mode where you can share a room and draw with other people. You can see everyone's strokes and cursors as you work, so there's officially no excuse for arguing over who drew that weird-looking thing that just appeared on the canvas.

## 📱 Make yourself at home

The interface adapts to different screen sizes, and you can draw with a mouse or use a touchscreen on supported devices. There are quite a few visual effects, but if your device starts struggling to keep up, you can turn off **Liquid Glass** in the settings.

## ❤️ Now it's your turn

I've put a lot of time into making this more than just a blank canvas, and I'm genuinely excited for you to try it out. Explore, mix and match brushes, draw whatever comes to mind, and export your creations. You don't have to be good at drawing to have fun with it. In fact, some of the funniest results happen when you have absolutely no idea what you're doing.
